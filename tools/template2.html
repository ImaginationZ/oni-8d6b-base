Twin-Spine 8+6 Base

Built on 大叔追云彩's 8+6 semi-open base. The schedules do the sharing: 4 dupe shifts of 2 and 3 bionic shifts of 2, with no beds for the bionics. Each storey is 4 tiles tall around one central shaft. Dupes leave through the middle storey: they walk past the printing pod in a nature reserve, put on a suit, and step onto a spine that runs from the bottom of the map to space.

Schedules

Dupes have 4 shifts of 2. Each shift sleeps first, then rests, and the shifts start 5 hours apart, so no two shifts are ever asleep at the same time. The table, toilets and rec room see at most 2 dupes at once.

Bionics use a 3-cycle schedule. Each pair rests for 6 hours on its own cycle and works the rest of the time. There's no Bedtime, because bionics have no stamina and defragmenting is optional. That frees all their bed space.

Getting out

  • Inside: a fire pole for going down and a plastic ladder for going up. Each storey has a door on both sides of the shaft.
  • Exit (F3): shaft → nature reserve → door → checkpoint → 4 docks → mechanized airlock → spine.
  • Spines: a ladder and fire pole on each outer wall, running from the space surface to the bottom of the map.
  • Tubes: one transit tube station per side at airlock level, with the tube heading west or east. The rows above and below stay clear for more tube lines.
  • Idle dupes late-game wait at the checkpoint and tie up docks. Following his note, either build a gym of manual generators outside, or enable the checkpoint only when a duplicant sensor detects someone.

Utilities

Gas

  • O₂ from the SPOM goes up the shaft wall to one vent per wing, to the 8 docks and to the canister filler.
  • Demand is about 0.8 kg/s for dupes and 0.6 kg/s for bionics. Two electrolyzers make 1.78 kg/s.
  • H₂ goes to the 2 generators on F5. CO₂ collects on F6, where a mini pump with an element sensor sends it out.
  • Natural gas feeds the gas range.

Liquid

  • Clean water from outside feeds the electrolyzers, lavatories, sinks, espresso machine and soda fountain.
  • Polluted water goes to the sieve on F6 and comes back clean. Gunk is piped out to a refinery.
  • Phyto or crude oil goes to the lube station.
  • Cooling: an aquatuner in the steam chamber cools a radiant pipe loop through the floors of F4 to F6. The turbine above the chamber turns that heat back into power.

Power and conveyors

  • 2 × 800 W hydrogen plus the turbine (up to 850 W). Import the rest over a heavy-watt line into F5.
  • Power-bank loop: the loader on F5 sends empty banks to the chargers, and charged banks go to smart storage.
  • Food: a conveyor runs up the west spine to the kitchen receptacle. Filter it to finished dishes only, with no raw ingredients, eggshells, polluted dirt or rot.
  • Re-sync the power-bank and lube-door timing every 500 cycles or so, because they drift (his note).

Check in game before you commit

  • BNI import is untested. SpiceGrinder, CraftingTable and Headquarters (the printing pod) are the least certain IDs.
  • Gunk extractor: if the game counts it as industrial machinery and the washroom stops working, swap it with the canister filler on F5.
  • Checkpoints: both should face outward, with the docks between the checkpoint and the airlock. The west one is flipped (orientation 5).
  • Steam turbine: it sits in the F5 floor row and forms the ceiling of the steam chamber. Confirm the game allows this placement.
  • Printing pod: the game places it at world start. To use this layout, build the base around your existing pod, or plan the shaft so the west nature reserve lands on it.
