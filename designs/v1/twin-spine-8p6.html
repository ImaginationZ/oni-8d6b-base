Twin-Spine 8+6 Base

An end-game living block on a strict 4-tile storey grid. One central shaft serves seven storeys. The middle storey is the only way out: dupes walk through a nature reserve, put on an atmo suit, and step onto an exterior spine that runs from the bottom of the map to space, with a transit tube station on each side.

One table, four schedules

The communal table seats 3. Split the 8 dupes into groups of 3, 3 and 2, and start each group's day one hour after the previous one. Each group eats in the first Downtime hour after waking (outlined), so at most 3 dupes are at the table at once. The same offset spreads out the toilets, sinks and rec room.

Getting out

  • Inside: fire pole (fast down) and plastic ladder (fast up) in a 2-wide shaft. Every storey has a door on both sides of the shaft.
  • Exit (F4): shaft → nature reserve → door → checkpoint → 6 docks → mechanized airlock → exterior spine. Coming back is the same path in reverse, and dupes drop their suits before the checkpoint.
  • Spines: ladder and fire pole pairs just outside the west and east walls. Extend them all the way to the space surface and the bottom of the map.
  • Tubes: one transit tube access on each side, at airlock level, already suited. The tube leaves above the station and heads west or east. The 5 tiles above and below each station stay clear for more tube lines.
  • Both exits reach both directions. If one side runs out of suits, pathing sends dupes to the other side.

Utilities

Gas

  • O₂: the SPOM pumps feed a pipe up the west shaft wall to one vent per wing near the shaft door, plus branches to the 12 docks and the canister filler. Set an atmo sensor so vents stop at about 1.8 kg.
  • O₂ demand is about 0.8 kg/s for dupes and 0.6 kg/s for bionic canisters. 2 electrolyzers make 1.78 kg/s.
  • H₂ (224 g/s) goes up to the 2 generators (200 g/s, 1.6 kW). Send the surplus out of the base.
  • CO₂ sinks to F7. Put a mini gas pump with a CO₂ element sensor at the bottom of the shaft and pipe it out, for example to a slickster ranch.
  • Natural gas to the gas range (100 g/s while cooking).

Liquid

  • Clean water in: electrolyzers (2 kg/s), lavatories, sinks, espresso, soda fountain. Bring it from outside; the toilet loop alone is far too small.
  • Polluted water from lavatories and sinks goes to the sieve on F7, which returns clean water to the same line.
  • The gunk extractor pipe leads out of the base, to an oil or gunk refinery.
  • Phyto oil or crude oil goes to the lubrication station. Crude oil gives a bigger buff.
  • Cooling: an aquatuner in the insulated steam chamber under the turbine runs one coolant loop of radiant pipe through the floor rows of F7, F6 and F5 (electrolyzers, chargers, lube station, range). The turbine's water output goes back into the chamber.

Power, solids, logic

  • Generation: 2 × 800 W hydrogen plus the turbine (up to 850 W). The heavy draws are chargers (about 240 W average each), sun lamp 960 W, aquatuner 1.2 kW, soda and espresso 480 W each, and the arcade. Bring a heavy-watt line from your main grid into F6.
  • F6 east qualifies as a Power Plant room, so a Power Control Station there can boost the generators.
  • Raw ingredients come in on a conveyor rail that runs up the west spine and through the wall into the kitchen receptacle.
  • Smart storage next to the chargers holds charged power banks, so bionics pick them up in the same trip.

Check in game before you commit

  • BNI import is untested. The file matches the format BNI's API returns, but I couldn't upload it to confirm. SpiceGrinder and CraftingTable aren't in BNI's old public database, so those two IDs are the least certain.
  • Gunk extractor and the washroom. The wiki says bionics get the washroom bonus from an extractor inside a Washroom, but doesn't say whether the extractor counts as industrial machinery. If the room loses its type, swap the extractor with the canister filler on F6.
  • Checkpoint direction. The west checkpoint is flipped (orientation 5). Both should point outward, with the docks between the checkpoint and the airlock.
  • Steam turbine. It sits in the F6 floor row, so its bottom row forms the ceiling of the steam chamber. That's the usual build, but confirm the game accepts the placement.
  • Wild plants in pots. The nature reserves use 4 floor pots and 2 hanging pots. Plant wild-type decor plants, such as Mirth Leaf or Bluff Briar, and don't fertilize them.
  • I couldn't watch the reference videos. 大叔追云彩 hasn't released the layout document, so this is my own design, not a copy of theirs. It follows the conventions the videos' titles and the BNI 8-dupe bases share: a central shaft, 16-wide wings with 64-tile rooms, and a single banquet hall.
