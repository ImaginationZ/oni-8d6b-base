Semi-Open 8d6b Base

Modelled on 拿个破笔水's semi-open base. Dupes and bionics wear atmo suits everywhere except inside two Spacefarer Modules, which hold the 8 comfy beds. One central shaft is the nature reserve and the way up to space, down to the bottom of the map, and out to the transit tube. Living rooms are on the left; utilities and the bedroom corridor are on the right.

Dupe loop

4 shifts of 2. Each shift sleeps first, then rests; sleep times are staggered, so at most 2 dupes are out of their suits at once.

  1. Shaft (nature reserve, +6 morale).
  2. Into the bedroom corridor (R2): the checkpoint takes the suit; 5 docks hold the sleepers' suits.
  3. Sleep in a habitat (4 comfy beds each).
  4. Suit back on, back to the shaft.
  5. Washroom (L2) across the shaft, one way right to left: lavatory, then sink.
  6. Meal from the fridge window by the banquet door; the kitchen below feeds it by conveyor.
  7. Banquet hall: the one communal table.
  8. Ladder up to recreation (L4); massage clinic above it for stressed dupes.
  9. Back into the shaft: ladder or tube up, fire pole down, to work.

Bionic loop

3 shifts of 2, no beds: each bionic rests 6 h once every 3 cycles, staggered 6 h apart. They never take their suits off.

  1. Shaft.
  2. Same washroom: the 2 gunk extractors are first from the door.
  3. Through the banquet hall, up to the ledge, east door (bionics only) into the bionic room (L3).
  4. Lube station, charged power banks (belt-fed from the 4 chargers in R1), high-pressure O₂ vent.
  5. Ladder up to recreation, then out through the shaft.

The only bionic ban is the bedroom corridor door. Everything else is shared, as in 拿个破笔水's semi-open base.

Check in game before you commit

  • Habitat bonuses: does the bedroom count as Luxury Barracks inside a Spacefarer Module? Hover the interior.
  • O₂ into the habitats: route not drawn yet (their interior has a gas vent fed from outside).
  • Clear space above a module: the gym sits right on top, as in 拿个破笔水's base.
  • Wild plants in pots counting for the nature reserve, and a frozen banquet hall keeping its decor and recreation requirements.
  • Crossing the shaft: dupes step between the ladders and the pole sideways; doors sit next to ladders so nobody relies on it to get in or out.
